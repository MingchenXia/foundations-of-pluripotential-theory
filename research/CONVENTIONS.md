# Sample convention contract

The reader-facing global list is copied verbatim from the sample. Definitions introduced later in that sample stay at their natural first-use locations in the new book; do not add undeclared line bundles or Lelong symbols to the front matter merely to collect formulas.

| Convention | Exact sample location | Consequence to preserve |
|---|---|---|
| dd^c = i/(2pi) partial barpartial | author/conventions.tex; chapter_pshfunctions thm:psh_rigid | dd^c log\|z\|² = [0] on C; coefficients for Laplacian/current conversions must be explicit |
| nu(log\|z\|²,0)=1 | rmk:Lelongconv; eq:nuvarphix | Compute divisorial coefficients using squared logarithms |
| I(phi): \|f\|² exp(-phi) locally integrable | def:pshfunctions:L1122; rmk:misconv | I(lambda log\|z\|²)=(z^floor(lambda)); DEL's exp(-2 weight) uses half-weight |

| Analytic singularities: rational c>0, bounded remainder | def:neatanasing | Neat means smooth remainder; gentle is a separate definition |
| Psh is pointwise; all components of all line sections | def:psh, thm:psh_rigid | A positive distribution determines an a.e. class and its canonical psh representative, not arbitrary point modifications |
| Dimension zero | def:psh; first examples | Psh on the singleton is a real value; Lelong number there is zero |
| Willard subnets, monotonicity not strict, N includes zero | author/conventions.tex | Preserve directed order/finality in every net argument |
| Ordinary, P and I singularity orders differ | chapter_comp, sec:PIpartialorder | Never identify their equivalences without a theorem |
| Quasi-suprema for differences at common poles | def:quasi_sup | State the locus where subtraction is defined and justify exceptional-set removal |

No notation change is inferred from any newly supplied PDF, its annotations or its errata. Record source conversions at the calculation that needs them.
