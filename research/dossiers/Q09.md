## Proof architecture

For local psh u_i, restrict the bounded products of max(u_i,-k) to O_k=intersection{u_i>-k}; plurifine locality makes these positive coefficient measures increase. Uniform local trace mass is precisely the finiteness condition. Prove independence under bounded pluriharmonic changes on compact chart neighborhoods, and prove multilinearity on common superlevel sets where every potential and sum is finite. The product assigns every pluripolar set zero mass by the bounded-potential capacity result.

Closedness needs a gradient/chain-rule lemma for bounded psh functions against an arbitrary fixed closed positive current of bidimension (1,1). Construct gradients in the coefficient-matrix Hilbert seminorm and use Q08 polynomial weighted continuity to prove their strong convergence under smooth decreasing approximation. This justifies both chain rule and Cauchy–Schwarz without importing a Sobolev theorem for singular currents. For u=sum u_i<=0, take w_k=chi(exp(u/k)) and a second cutoff theta(w_k). Their derivative errors vanish by dominated convergence against the product trace, while the gradient energies stay bounded by integration by parts and the assumed restricted mass.

For the global bound first prove the bounded-background cohomological Stokes formula. If theta_i is not positive, enlarge it to tau_i=theta_i+C*omega, use globally positive tau_i+ddc max(phi_i,-k), and compare on common finite levels. Do not treat theta_i+ddc max(phi_i,-k) as globally positive under a merely smooth background.
