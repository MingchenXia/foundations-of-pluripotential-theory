# P02 — Harmonic functions and the Dirichlet problem

The active chapter includes all real dimensions d ≥ 1, distributional Weyl regularity, normalized fundamental solutions, the ball Poisson formula, Harnack chains, smooth convergence, continuous-data Perron resolutivity, barriers, harmonic measure, and Green functions on bounded regular domains with regular-domain exhaustion.

Checked published locators in the retained 2007 reprint of Hörmander: Theorems 3.1.2–3.1.7, pp. 117–123; Perron replacement, Theorem 3.3.3, Definition 3.3.4, Theorem 3.3.5, pp. 171–174; local barrier gluing before 3.4.16, p. 218; barrier equivalence in Theorem 3.4.17, pp. 219–220. These locators do not certify the surrounding capacity or exceptional-set claims. The source's fundamental solution solves ΔE = δ; the book's positive Green kernel solves −ΔG = δ. The homogeneous Weyl proof uses radial mollifiers and does not import analytic continuation.

The unsigned student write-up of Tobias Mai's 2020 course was read for harmonic/Poisson statements and proofs in Chapters II–IV, pp. 9–25. Its Theorem IV.6 on printed/PDF p. 24 has r^(N+2) in the upper Harnack coefficient. Direct inspection of the original PDF confirms the typo. At the center that coefficient becomes r^4, so a positive constant harmonic function on a ball with r < 1 contradicts it. The Poisson-kernel ratio gives r^(N−2). Other source slips noticed during the read include “holomorphic” for “harmonic” in III.8 and sign/integral-bound slips in IV.4; the book supplies its own proofs and uses the published Hörmander locators for attribution.

Scope: Green symmetry and arbitrary Greenian-domain theory are not asserted. Prove symmetry before using a general-domain Green energy. Perron equality for continuous data does not imply boundary regularity; the punctured-ball example records the distinction. No Guedj–Zeriahi assertion is used in this chapter, so no errata candidate has been adjudicated or silently adopted.
