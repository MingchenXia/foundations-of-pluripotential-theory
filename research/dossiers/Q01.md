The chapter reconstructs exterior calculus and Stokes, positivity cones and their interior, order-zero estimates, local potentials, holomorphic logarithms and the divisor current, and the precise domains of current maps. Its dependencies are P01–P03. The analytic-set and weighted-L2 chapters are not prerequisites.

The local-potential proof uses the scalar fundamental solution for the trace, closure to make the trace-free remainder harmonic, the smooth real Poincare homotopy, and a proved smooth dbar lemma. This avoids importing a distributional Dolbeault theorem or using the later psh characterization. In Demailly's III §1.18, the displayed expression following v minus its conjugate has a real/imaginary-part inconsistency in the extracted text; our calculation is made directly, with a real primitive and potential 4 pi Im(f).

Poincare–Lelong is proved from contour preparation, elementary square-free polynomial factorization over a fraction field, and coordinate slices. A basis of directions with nonzero leading Taylor polynomial makes each coordinate projection of the exceptional divisor locus negligible. The diagonal Hessian measures give that locus zero mass. The proof therefore does not use a general analytic stratification, a support theorem, or Skoda–El Mir extension. The integration current is defined independently on the local smooth divisor sheets before its equality with dd^c log|f|^2 is established.

Normalization: Demailly III (2.15) writes (i/pi) partial barpartial log|f|. This equals our (i/(2 pi)) partial barpartial log|f|^2. His subsequent dd^c operator is twice ours; formulas involving it must be converted separately. His bidimension notation is converted to our bidegree notation.

Holomorphic pullback of a positive closed (1,1)-current uses canonical local potentials only where their compositions are not identically minus infinity on a source component. Submersion pullback and proper-on-support pushforward of general currents have separate constructions. No arbitrary pullback or singular wedge product is asserted.
