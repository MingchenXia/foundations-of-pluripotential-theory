Proof interfaces requiring particular care:

- Mean slopes establish additivity of Lelong numbers; a supremum-growth statement alone does not justify addition of two suprema.
- Strong Noetherianity uses finite hypersurface projection, coefficient division and companion matrices to justify coherence of its exact finite direct image. It does not invoke a general coherent direct-image theorem.
- Coherence uses finite-jet interpolation and Krull closure before any openness or Bergman approximation result. Uniform Hilbert generation is then proved by a complete-metric-space argument and bounded coefficient normal families.
- Siu follows from the strict lower and upper multiplier-ideal order bounds and an analytic jet intersection. Arbitrary analytic intersections are justified by finite germ generation and the identity theorem on irreducible representatives.
- The strong-openness curve comes from the blowup of (F,g), followed by internal curve selection. Quotients only need be holomorphic on the normalized parameter disc. A coefficient/root-average argument gives the uniform |a|⁻² norm obstruction.
- Increasing-weight stability uses induction on dimension and a fixed inner slice polydisc. A finite cover of its compact closure supplies a common index before dominated convergence. The moving-hyperplane extension has a uniform normal-radius bound. No arbitrary L1 weight stability is claimed.
- The valuative converse chooses one sufficiently large m, sets φ_m=m⁻¹ log Σ|g_i|² for generators of I(mφ), and uses exp(m(φ_m−φ))∈L1. On φ<pφ_m the comparison (m−1)φ−mφ_m≤0 holds when p≥m/(m−1) and φ_m≤0. This is the precise non-asymptotic transfer step.
