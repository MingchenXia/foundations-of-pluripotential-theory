# Q07: global potentials and positive classes

The active coherent core proves the compact Kähler global dd^c lemma and current potentials, normalized compactness and minimal singularities, cone topology, divisorial decomposition, modified nefness, minimal multiplicities, positivity loci and their behavior under modifications. The final section supplies quadratic psh metrics, curvature, the line-bundle Poincaré–Lelong formula and rational section-defined Fubini–Study operations. Q07-05 is not complete: compact positive-metric extension with uniform constants and global section Bergman approximation still require their own proofs.

## Internal proof architecture

The global dd^c proof uses a scalar Poisson equation for the trace. Fourier Sobolev embedding and compactness, a difference-quotient regularity proof, duality for the shifted form Laplacian and mean-zero scalar inversion are proved here. A closed primitive real (1,1)-current is coclosed by the explicit Kähler star identity, hence smooth harmonic. The trace convention is Lambda dd^c f = -Lf/(4 pi), with L the nonnegative Riemannian Laplacian. No global Hodge or dd^c theorem is silently imported.

Normalized compactness propagates local noncollapse across the connected finite chart cover. Supremum continuity includes moving maximum points. A weighted decreasing sum proves the non-pluripolar compact-set normalization bound. Smooth-target modification descent uses bounded removal across the codimension-two exceptional image and canonical representatives, without the unaccepted singular-space disc criterion. Divisorial Lelong bounds use L1 continuity of pullback on the normalized compact families.

Cone closure uses the exact trace mass and weak compactness. Unit multiplier ideals make the Q06 cutoff-sum approximants smooth; strict curvature is retained by changing the background before approximation. Divisorial subtraction uses logarithmic growth, bounded removal and the measure restriction on the singular divisor locus. Uniqueness checks the measure of the infinite series of other divisors on the chosen divisor, rather than treating pointwise avoidance of a countable list as sufficient.

Minimal multiplicities use negative curvature allowances at boundary classes. Equality with the positive minimal-current number is proved only for big classes by a convex combination with a strict current. Modified-nef closure uses small harmonic class representatives, not semicontinuity of Lelong numbers in a weak limit. The non-Kähler locus is realized by a finite maximum after coherent-ideal stationarity; no claim about smoothness of that maximum is needed. The allowance identity is an intersection, with the non-pseudo-effective and non-big conventions stated. Pushforward of modified nef classes is reduced to the big case and non-divisorial minimal currents.

The section metric convention remains quadratic, exp(-phi), with rational analytic coefficients. Clearing tensor degrees proves closure under rational finite convex combinations and minima. Real convex parameters are not silently substituted. Source findings record the disconnected compactness normalization and the sample's unspecified convex parameter.
