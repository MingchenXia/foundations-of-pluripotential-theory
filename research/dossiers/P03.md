# P03 — Subharmonic functions

The proof order is local sphere inequality → bounded harmonic comparison → all-radius means → local integrability → compact-support fundamental potentials → distributional characterization → pointwise uniqueness and Riesz decomposition → compactness/Hartogs → countable envelope selection → local polar removability. This order avoids importing capacity or regularity of arbitrary envelopes into the representative theorem.

Checked Hörmander locators: §3.2, pp. 141–151, especially 3.2.3, 3.2.8–3.2.13, with their proofs; §3.4, pp. 203–206, Definition 3.4.1, Theorem 3.4.3 and Proposition 3.4.4. The book expands the local integral-bound propagation omitted in the source compactness proof and constructs general real distribution representatives through Riesz potentials and Weyl's lemma. The compactness theorem's distributional-to-L1 implication has an explicit common local upper bound. Higher Sobolev/weighted convergence claims are outside this interface.

The class includes −∞ on components. The Riesz measure is Δu, so log|z| has mass 2π and log|z|² has mass 4π. An arbitrary supremum is asserted measurable for completed Lebesgue measure; Borel measurability is not presumed. Empty envelopes, the one-dimensional convex case and nontriviality in the removal proof are handled explicitly.

P03 proves that a locally bounded upper envelope equals its regularization almost everywhere. Proving its discrepancy set polar is assigned to P04-02 together with capacity and finite-energy measures. This is a dependency refinement, not a claim that the missing stronger theorem is false or open in the literature. No supplied GZ or errata claim is used here.
