# P01 — Measures and distributions

The active chapter proves Euclidean Radon regularity, positive-functional representation, completed-product integration, distributional smoothing, positive distributions as measures, vague/narrow convergence, countable measure selection, Willard subnet compactness and Arzelà–Ascoli. Undergraduate Lebesgue integration is the reader prerequisite; the convergence and product results actually used are nevertheless proved.
