# Plurisubharmonic functions

Q02 follows the usual sample definition and places the automatic USC theorem immediately beside it. The finite-nonnegative induction reconstructs PSHD Lemma 1.2 (pp. 1–2) and Proposition 2.1 (pp. 4–5): truncated fiber means prove Borelness before P06's incidence bound is applied. Q01/P03 already give a canonical ordinary subharmonic representative of a positive Hessian distribution, with holomorphic compositions subharmonic or identically minus infinity; this establishes the representative needed here without using the theorem being proved. Polar coordinates identify the given finite line-subharmonic function pointwise, and truncation gives the full PSHD theorem (p. 6).

Additional interfaces: ordinary subharmonicity, local integrability, positive-Hessian characterization with its canonical representative; holomorphic maps/manifolds; finite maxima and boundary gluing; decreasing nets, L1 compactness/Hartogs, Choquet envelopes; bounded extension across a locally contained holomorphic zero locus; a thin-set unbounded extension criterion; Kiselman minimum with a precise invariant-exhaustion hypothesis and product cases. Any use of analytic codimension requires A01's actual dimension estimate, not the sample proof's unproved directional-projection claim.

Source scopes newly read: sample `chapter_pshfunctions.tex`, def:psh, thm:psh_rigid, prop:func_domain, thm:GRexten and its proof, prop:Kis; PSHD pp. 1–2 and 4–6; Demailly author-hosted 2012 manuscript I §7.B (7.5), p. 57, statement and proof. Demailly's general tube theorem depends on an invariant exhaustion supplied by pseudoconvex boundary-distance theory. That dependency must be proved and placed in Q04 before the full pseudoconvex formulation is used; a product-tube proof alone is not a proof of the general statement. The smooth minimum's Schur-complement calculation and an explicit invariant-exhaustion approximation are reconstructed in Q02. Crossref metadata independently confirms Kiselman's 1978 article DOI 10.1007/BF01403083, Inventiones 49(2), 137–148; the article's full proof has not yet been read.
